QuickBooks Integrations

B2B Invoice Automation

✓ Production-verified

Problem

A dedicated BPO ($10/hr) manually generated wholesale invoices daily, frequently missing invoices, making errors, delaying book closing. No system connected Shopify fulfillments to QuickBooks automatically.

What I built

An app that runs hourly: checks Shopify for fulfilled orders, cross-references Extensiv WMS, and auto-generates accurate invoices in QuickBooks Online, with built-in accuracy validation. As B2B grew, pre-order and back-order invoicing got far more complex. The app absorbed it. What used to take hours now runs in the background.

Ops Automation

QVC Drop Ship Automation

✓ Production-verified

Problem

Every QVC order required manually logging into Rithum, downloading files, reformatting in Excel, re-entering into Extensiv, and attaching labels. Orders not fulfilled within 48 hours triggered chargebacks, a constant pressure with no margin for error.

What I built

An hourly Claude pipeline that monitors Rithum for new orders, auto-generates shipping labels, converts and imports orders into Extensiv, and sends Slack notifications to the warehouse, with fault-tolerant retry logic and accuracy validation throughout.

Pricing Strategy

Wholesale Pricing Framework & 7-Tier PIP

Problem

Wholesale shipping was charged at ad-hoc warehouse pass-through rates, with no standardization, no volume incentives. With 72 accounts at the time, every retailer was being treated the same regardless of order size, and there was no framework to reward loyalty or growth.

What I built

Designed Clove's wholesale shipping pricing from scratch: modeling real cost data into a tiered structure, then layering a Partnership Incentive Program on top to drive account growth.

Solution — two interlocking frameworks

Volume-based shipping model

  • Built on actual UPS Ground cost data
  • 7 price tiers, $5.00/pair (1–3) to $2.00/pair (90+)
  • Non-shoe orders at 10% of order value; SLAs by order size

Partnership Incentive Program

  • 5 revenue tiers, Partner ($5K) to Platinum ($100K)
  • 2–7% order discounts; free shipping at Gold and above
  • Quarterly Co-Op credits ($500–$2,000), SPIFFs, staff pull contests
  • Defined payment terms, MAP compliance, eligibility rules

Impact

264%
order volume growth post-PIP (Apr 2025–Jun 2026: 164 orders/mo avg vs. 45)
78%
accounts with fill-in orders (123/157)
5 tiers
Partner ($5K) to Platinum ($100K)
